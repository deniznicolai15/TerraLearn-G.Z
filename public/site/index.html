How to Get to Mt. Pamitinan

Complete trail guide — Montalban, Rizal

Step 1: Getting to Montalban

From Manila, take a jeepney or UV Express from Cubao (Aurora Blvd.) bound for Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal. The ride takes approximately 1 to 1.5 hours depending on traffic. You can also drive via Marcos Highway or Batasan Road and head toward Montalban town proper.

Step 2: Registration at the Brgy. Hall

Upon arriving at Montalban, proceed to Barangay San Rafael. You need to register at the Barangay Hall and pay the environmental fee. This is also where you can hire a local guide, which is required for all hikers. Make sure to bring a valid ID for registration.

Step 3: The Jump-Off Point

From the Barangay Hall, you will walk or take a tricycle to the Wawa Dam area which serves as the jump-off point. The scenic Wawa Dam is the starting landmark. Cross the hanging bridge and follow the trail markers along the riverbank.

Step 4: The Trail to the Summit

The trail is a mix of river trekking, rock scrambling, and jungle hiking. The total hike to the summit takes about 2 to 3 hours at a moderate pace. There are steep rocky sections that require using ropes (usually set up by your guide). The difficulty is rated 4/9 on the Philippine mountaineering scale.

Step 5: At the Summit

The summit offers a panoramic view of Montalban, the Sierra Madre mountain range, Wawa Dam, and Metro Manila in the distance. The summit features the famous Pamitinan Cave, which holds historical significance as the site where Andres Bonifacio and the Katipuneros shouted "Long Live the Philippines!" in 1896.

Step 6: Return Trip

Descend via the same trail back to the Wawa Dam area. The descent typically takes 1.5 to 2 hours. You can cool off at the Wawa Dam or the Daranak Falls nearby before heading back to Manila.

What to Bring

  • At least 2 liters of water
  • Trail food and packed lunch
  • Comfortable hiking shoes with good grip
  • Rain jacket or poncho (during wet season)
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent
  • Extra clothes and towel
  • Flashlight or headlamp
  • First aid kit
  • Trash bag (Leave No Trace)
  • Valid ID for registration

Important Reminders

  • Always hike with a registered local guide
  • Check weather conditions before your trip
  • Do not litter — practice Leave No Trace
  • Respect the local community and wildlife
  • Best months to visit: November to May (dry season)
